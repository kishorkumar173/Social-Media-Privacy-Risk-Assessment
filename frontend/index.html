Account Security

Objective: Protect systems, credentials, and accounts from unauthorized access, breach, or compromise.

  • Multi-Factor Authentication (TOTP / FIDO2 Keys)
  • Unique 16+ character passphrases via Password Manager
  • Unrecognized device login alerts & session termination
"An account can have an unbreakable password, yet still expose an individual to physical harm or extortion."
Personal Privacy

Objective: Control how personal information is collected, correlated, shared, and exposed to the public.

  • Concealing personal phone numbers, birth year, and residential cues
  • Preventing real-time venue broadcasting & vacation countdowns
  • Restricting timeline tagging and external search engine indexing
"Privacy controls define your digital exposure surface; Security controls protect access to it."
