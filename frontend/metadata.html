Why EXIF Metadata Matters in Cybersecurity

Exchangeable Image File Format (EXIF) is automatically embedded by modern smartphones and digital cameras into every photo taken.

  • GPS Coordinates: Exact latitude and longitude pinpoints residences, workplaces, or schools.
  • Device Identifiers: Make, model, firmware version, and lens specifications can assist hardware fingerprinting.
  • Timestamps: Date and second-level time tags disclose daily schedules and travel itineraries.
